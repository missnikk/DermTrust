## 1. Executive Summary & Legal Clearance

DermTrust is strictly an academic, educational, and research project. All training and evaluation datasets used across the project lifecycle have been audited for:
1. **Legal Provenance & Usage Rights:** Permissibility for academic non-commercial model development.
2. **Attribution Requirements:** Ensuring upstream primary sources receive due academic credit.
3. **Partition Integrity:** Patient-level isolation to prevent train/test data leakage.
4. **Demographic Equity:** Empirical representation across Fitzpatrick Skin Types (FST I through VI), with special attention to dark skin tones (FST IV–VI).
5. **DWH Lineage:** Complete traceability from Bronze raw downloads to Gold clean training tensors.

## 3. Deep Dive: Upstream License Terms & Legal Boundaries

### 3.1 Fitzpatrick 17k Dataset
- **Upstream Repository:** `github.com/nick-groh/fitzpatrick17k`
- **License Terms:** Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0).
- **Compliance Analysis:**
  - *Attribution:* Fully satisfied in project documentation and in-app About / Academic Disclosure dialog (`Groh et al., CVPR 2021`).
  - *Non-Commercial:* DermTrust is an open-source, non-monetized undergraduate academic engineering thesis. No advertising, subscription, or commercial monetization is present.
  - *ShareAlike:* Any derived dataset metadata or public manifests conform to CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 licensing requirements.
  - *APK Inclusion:* Only trained neural network parameters (floating-point tensors) derived through mathematical gradient descent are packaged within the Android assets. Raw clinical patient photographs are never bundled into the APK.

### 3.2 SkinDiseaseBD Deduplication & Integrity Finding
- **Source:** Mendeley Data (`10.17632/9ggd3shdr7.3`).
- **Initial Observation:** Archive claimed 1,612 images (`Images_512x512_v2.zip`).
- **Forensic Audit Finding:** File-level perceptual hashing and metadata analysis revealed that the 1,612 images were generated by the original publishers applying 8x offline augmentations (`aug_0_`, `aug_1_`, ..., `aug_4_`) to only **197 unique clinical patient encounters**.
- **Corrective Action Implemented:** All pre-augmented duplicate files were stripped out of the validation and test sets. Only primary un-augmented patient encounters were retained in evaluation partitions to eliminate artificial data leakage and inflated accuracy claims.

### 3.3 Rejection of Local Melasma Cohort
- **Cohort Details:** 48 local high-resolution clinical melasma photographs located in author archives.
- **Audit Decision:** **REJECTED FROM DEPLOYED MANIFESTS**.
- **Reason:** Lack of formal institutional review board (IRB) ethics approval and verifiable, signed patient informed-consent documentation. To maintain absolute academic and medical data governance integrity, these 48 images were quarantined into an isolated folder and excluded from the active production model weights.

### 4.2 Independent Verification & Fairness Test Benchmark (N = 600)
To establish defensible, tamper-proof performance across diverse demographics, a dedicated 600-image benchmark was curated with strict 50:50 parity:
- **Light Skin Group (FST I–III):** 300 cases (50 per disease class + 50 OOD)
- **Dark Skin Group (FST IV–VI):** 300 cases (50 per disease class + 50 OOD)
- **Total Test Cases:** Exactly 600 verified samples.
- **Partition Integrity:** Zero overlap with training patient identifiers (100% patient-level holdout).

## 5. Data Warehouse (DWH) Four-Tier Lineage Architecture

The project data management adheres to a 4-tier Medallion Data Lakehouse architecture:

```
[ Tier 1: Bronze (Raw) ]
   ├── downloads/fitzpatrick17k/ (Raw CSV & web-scraped images)
   ├── downloads/memi_ds/ (Raw clinical archives)
   └── downloads/mendeley_skindisease/ (Raw archives)
           │
           ▼
[ Tier 2: Silver (Cleaned & Normalized) ]
   ├── Resolution normalized to 224x224 RGB
   ├── Perceptual hashing (pHash) applied to remove exact and near duplicates
   └── Corrupted EXIF data and truncated JPEGs purged
           │
           ▼
[ Tier 3: Gold (Partitioned & Balanced) ]
   ├── Patient-aware stratified partitioning (Train 70%, Val 15%, Test 15%)
   ├── Demographic stratification across FST groups
   └── Manifests: data/processed/gold_train.csv, gold_val.csv, gold_test.csv
           │
           ▼
[ Tier 4: Platinum (Inference & Embedded Assets) ]
   ├── Quantized TFLite models (`dermtrust_classifier.tflite`)
   ├── Offline Grad-CAM weights (`cam_weights.json`)
   └── On-device evaluation benchmarks (`data/test_benchmark_600.json`)
```

## 6. Known Dataset Limitations & Governance Boundaries

1. **Retrospective Web Scraping in Fitzpatrick 17k:** A portion of the original Fitzpatrick 17k dataset was assembled from clinical search engine indices (DermaAmin, Atlas Dermatologico). Image lighting, resolution, and focal distance vary significantly compared to controlled clinical trials.
2. **Subjective FST Annotations:** Fitzpatrick Skin Types in public dermatological benchmarks are often annotated post-hoc by non-dermatologist crowd workers or computed via Individual Typology Angle (ITA) heuristics rather than spectrophotometer readings.
3. **Melasma Sample Size:** Melasma is clinically diagnosed primarily on facial areas and occurs predominantly in Fitzpatrick types III–V. Lighter skin tone representations (FST I–II) for melasma in public datasets are naturally sparse.
4. **Clinical Scope:** The dataset exclusively supports screening decision support for the 5 target conditions and out-of-domain rejection. It does **not** evaluate skin malignancies (e.g., Melanoma, Basal Cell Carcinoma), and the application explicitly warns users regarding this limitation.
